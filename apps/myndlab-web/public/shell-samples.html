Myndlab app-shell animation — pick one

Pre-React loading shell. Renders for ~150–300 ms on every cold load. Currently a flat white prism. All four samples below use the brand palette (Aqua Cyan, Hot Magenta, Neon Yellow). Pure CSS, no JS, runs on the GPU compositor — < 1 KB added to the inline shell.

Variant A
Refraction pulse
Tri-color ring orbits the prism. A vertical color beam (cyan → magenta → yellow) sweeps across the prism every 3s. Matches brand book §2.4.
Variant B
Conic ring only
Static prism. Tri-color conic ring orbits at 1.5s. Simplest, cleanest. No beam, no spinning prism.
Variant C
Counter-rotate
Prism rotates 360° in 4s. Tri-color ring spins the opposite way in 2s. Architectural, no refraction.
Variant D
Orbiting dots
Three rings of cyan/magenta/yellow dots orbit the prism at different radii and speeds. More playful, less premium.
My pick: Variant A — Refraction pulse. Matches the brand book's "vertical beams refracted through prism geometry" language verbatim. Calm but alive — reads as "thinking" without being frantic. The other three are fine fallbacks if A feels too busy at small size.
